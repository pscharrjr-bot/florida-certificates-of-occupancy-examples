Florida Certificates of Occupancy: examples

New certificates of occupancy (CO), temporary COs and certificates of completion from official Florida open data (City of Orlando and Miami-Dade County), normalized into one weekly feed with address, residential/commercial, work type, valuation, square footage and builder.

Open on Apify Store

Privacy by design: owner names and personal owner fields are never collected or shown. Contractor names appear only when they're a business.
